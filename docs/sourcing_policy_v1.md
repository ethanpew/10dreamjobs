# Sourcing policy v1 (exploratory, first 100–200 roles)

Created 2026-10-13. Applies until you ask to refine it; each new policy is a new version, and every job records the version it was sourced under so cohorts can be compared.

- **Batch size:** 10 unique, verified-open postings. If fewer than 10 can be verified, the shortfall is reported and recorded on the batch.
- **Source preference:** employer career page or official ATS posting (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters), then reputable boards, always retaining the employer URL when one exists.
- **Verification:** posting URL loads, shows an apply path, and is not marked closed. Verification date stored per job.
- **Breadth targets per batch (when supply permits):** ≥ 5 role families, ≥ 5 industries, ≤ 2 roles per company, a mix of B2B/B2C, public/private/nonprofit/government, company sizes, cities/regions and remote/hybrid/on-site.
- **Fit mix:** resume fit sets relevance but is not the only criterion; each batch includes strong-fit and stretch roles (roughly half each at the start).
- **Seniority window:** Manager through VP/Head-of, with Senior IC and Senior Director as stretch; entry-level excluded unless you say otherwise.
- **No silent narrowing:** high ratings do not narrow sourcing before 100 completed ratings; narrowing happens only through "Refine sourcing".
- **After 100–200 ratings:** propose 70% aligned / 30% exploratory (adjustable).
- **Hard constraints:** none recorded. Pending your answers: geography and relocation, work authorization, exclusions, minimum compensation.
- **Role families:** Brand, Product Marketing, Consumer Insights, Marketing Research, Marketing Analytics, Growth, Lifecycle/CRM, Performance Marketing, Strategy, Marketing Operations, Partnerships, plus adjacent roles (e.g. UX/customer research, pricing, experimentation, marketing education/enrollment).
